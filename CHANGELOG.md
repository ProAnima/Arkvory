# Changelog

Entries for the next release, copied into its GitHub release notes when it is published. Published releases and their notes: [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ProAnima/Arkvory/releases).

## Next minor release

**Added**

- Console: a Modules & licence page for global administrators. It lists every module with its version, extension point, source and state, and the licences with their state and term. It only reads; modules are still installed and enabled on the server host.

**Changed**

- Console: a new look, "Indigo Ink". The navigation is a dark ink panel in both themes, with the chosen section marked by a lighter ground and an edge indicator; the accent is indigo, controls and cards are rounder and page titles larger. Every text keeps a contrast of at least 4.5:1 in the light and dark themes.
